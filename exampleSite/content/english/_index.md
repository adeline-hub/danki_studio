## Datify Gums pOps  
### International Trade, Gums & Data

*Datify Gums pOps* is a data‑driven exploration of global gum trade, combining
international trade statistics, botanical knowledge, and visual storytelling.

The project transforms raw trade flows into intuitive visual narratives,
revealing patterns of production, movement, value, and dependency across regions
and markets.

Bridging **economics, botany, and data visualization**, the work demonstrates how
carefully designed data systems can make complex global dynamics understandable
and actionable.
