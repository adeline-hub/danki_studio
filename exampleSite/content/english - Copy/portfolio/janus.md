## Janus Mediterranean Investment Analytics

### Context

This project was developed for **Janus Mediterranean**, an investment initiative focused on Mediterranean markets. The objective was to **analyze, structure, and visualize complex financial and impact data** to support strategic investment decisions.

The work combines financial performance indicators, geographic exposure, sector allocation, and impact metrics into a coherent analytics framework designed for clarity, comparability, and actionability.

---

## Project Type

**Professional**  
**Data Analytics · Investment Intelligence · Storytelling with Data**

---

## Goals

- Consolidate heterogeneous investment datasets into a single analytical framework  
- Reveal patterns across geography, sectors, and time  
- Enable decision-makers to explore performance and impact visually  
- Reduce analytical complexity while increasing insight density  

---

## Data

The analysis is based on internal Janus Mediterranean datasets, including:

- Investment allocations by country and sector  
- Financial performance indicators over time  
- Risk and exposure metrics  
- ESG and impact-related variables  

All data was cleaned, structured, and modeled to ensure consistency and analytical reliability.

---

## Approach

### 1. Understanding Data + Investment Objectives
The project started with a deep dive into the structure, granularity, and limitations of the available datasets, alongside discussions on investment goals, reporting needs, and strategic questions.

### 2. Modeling + Analytical Design
I designed a **lean data model** that supports both financial and impact analysis, enabling cross-reading between performance, geography, and sectoral exposure.

Key indicators were defined to ensure analytical coherence and comparability across time and regions.

### 3. Visualization + Iteration
A series of charts and dashboards were developed to surface insights clearly:
- Geographic distribution of investments
- Sector allocation and evolution
- Performance trends over time
- Comparative views across portfolios

Visuals were iterated collaboratively to ensure they supported real decision-making rather than static reporting.
