The Danki Process

1

Audit + Subtract

We don't start by adding; we start by deleting. I analyze your existing data architecture to identify "ROT" (Redundant, Obsolete, Trivial) data. We define the 3-5 metrics that actually drive your business goals, discarding the noise to reduce storage costs and cognitive load.

2

Architect + Build

I design the pipeline using the "Medallion" architecture. We use lightweight tools (Python, SQL, or Low-Code) to clean and structure your data. The goal is an eco-efficient system where data flows automatically from "Raw" to "Refined" without manual intervention.

3

Synthesize + Deliver

Data becomes insight. I deliver the final "Signal-Only" tool—whether it’s a minimalist dashboard, a pre-filled report, or an automated alert. We iterate until the output is zero-ambiguity, allowing you to make decisions in seconds, not hours.
